# Narration script — "Shifting to Retirement"

21-second data video, three 7-second scenes (30fps, hard cuts at frames 210 / 420).
Voiceover for each scene is timed to start a beat after the scene's cut so the
line lands as its graphic animates in.

---

### Scene 1 — Life Expectancy Countdown (0:00–0:07)
> "How long must your money last? A healthy fifty-five-year-old can expect to
> reach eighty-five — about thirty years."

*Syncs with:* the counter climbing 55 → 85 and the "30 Years of Retirement to
Fund" subtitle.

---

### Scene 2 — The Retirement Spending Smile (0:07–0:14)
> "But spending isn't flat. High in the go-go years, it dips mid-retirement,
> then climbs late for care. The retirement smile."

*Syncs with:* the smile curve drawing itself and the Go-Go / Slow-Go / No-Go
bands fading in.

---

### Scene 3 — Portfolio Growth Through Retirement (0:14–0:21)
> "Yet a disciplined portfolio, drawing four percent, keeps growing — past
> Medicare and RMDs — to near two point eight million."

*Syncs with:* the area chart rising, the Medicare (65) and RMD (73) milestone
callouts, and the ~$2.8M ending-value label.

---

**Disclaimer (on-screen, not narrated):** figures are illustrative estimates
assuming ~6–7% average annual return and a ~4% withdrawal rate — not a
guarantee of future performance.
